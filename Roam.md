## 🚌 The "Thandalam" Transport Hacks (Researcher's Note)
*Getting out of Thandalam is the first battle. Use these cheats:*
- **To T. Nagar:** Take Bus **554** or **591**. Direct connectivity to the city heart.
- **To Marina/San Thome:** Take Bus **25G** from Poonamallee Depot.

## ✈️ Zone 10: The Airport & Aerohub Experience
*You don't need a flight ticket to visit the mall outside.*

- [ ] **Aerohub Mall (Meenambakkam)** 💸💸
    - *Info:* Located *outside* the airport (West Block). No flight ticket needed.
    - *The Vibe:* 24/7 Food Court. You can sit here at 2 AM and watch travelers.
    - *Must Do:* Watch a late-night movie at **PVR Aerohub** (Brand new screens).
    - *Food:* **Junior Kuppanna** or **Burger King** (Open late).
    - *Transport Hack:* From Thandalam, take a share auto to **Poonamallee Depot**. Take Bus **154B** or **54** series to **Guindy/Airport**.
      
- [ ] **Chennai International Airport (T2/T4)** 💸
    - *Info:* You can't go inside terminals without a ticket, BUT you can go to the **Visitor Entry** area (check current rules) or just stand at the **Metro Station Bridge** connecting to the airport.
    - *The View:* The view of the runway and planes taking off from the Metro station glass walk is free and awesome.

- [ ] **Chennai Citi Centre (Mylapore)** 💸💸
    - *Info:* It’s not as huge as Phoenix, but it has the best "City View" from the food court.
    - *Must Do:* **INOX Cinema** (Top floor) - The screens are smaller but the crowd is decent.
    - *Food:* **Sangeetha Veg** (Ground floor) for class dosa, or the **Food Court (4th Floor)** which faces the Marina Beach lighthouse distance.
    - *The Hidden Gem:* The **Roof Top view** (accessible from parking/cinema side) lets you see the **Santhome Basilica** spires and the sea.
    - *Transport Hack (Student Budget):*
        1. Thandalam -> Poonamallee Depot.
        2. Bus **25G** (Direct to Marina/Light House).
        3. Get down at **Light House** stop. Walk 5 mins to the Mall.
        4. *Total Cost:* ₹45 (Bus) vs ₹600 (Uber).
